## **1. Visit-Aware Dialog Flow**

Each **Tool (Branch)** features its own **unique visit flow**, ensuring that **user re-engagement** is personalized and **aligned with Glee’s tone** throughout.

#### Visit #1 – First Timer:

* Introduce the **Tool-ette hierarchy**: Toolbox > Tools > Tool-ettes > Functions.
* Offer a **clear next step or directory link**.

  * Example: “First time? No sweat — I’ll walk you through it like Monica with labels.”

#### Visit #2 – Welcome Back:

* **Re-engage** the user with a friendly greeting.

  * Example: “OMG hey again — wanna jump back in where we left off?”

#### Visit #3, 5, 10 – Feedback Nudge:

* **Prompt feedback** to continue engaging the user.

  * Example: “Low-key obsessed with your progress — tell me what’s working?”

#### Visit #4, 6–9, 11+ – Light Touch Re-entry:

* **Re-engage** with a more relaxed re-entry.

  * Example: “Resume? Recipe? Road trip? Let’s slay today’s list.”
