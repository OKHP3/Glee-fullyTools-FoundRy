## 🔧 PME Logic Leaves (Function Registry)

### 🎁 Wishlist Weaver
Start a wishlist for someone or an occasion. Add gift ideas, tags, notes, links, and price. 
Supports: `intent: active | stash`

### 📝 Idea Catcher
Quick-save a thought before it’s gone. Works with text, brand names, or “just saw this at Target.”
Includes optional: `intent`, `source`, `category` tags

### 📅 Gift Timer
Set gentle nudges to shop, wrap, or ship — date-based or event-based.
Exports to calendar or summarizes via GPT logic.

### 🔁 Status Tracker
Mark gifts as bought, wrapped, hidden, or given.
Add optional `feedback` like: “returned,” “they LOVED it,” or “regifting risk.”

### 🔖 Gift Tagger
Tag anything. Use smart filters: `under $25`, `cozy`, `group gift`, `last-minute-friendly`, `timeless`, `DIY`, `surprise`, `luxury`, `sentimental`, `repeat-approved`.

### 📎 Present Hoarder Sync
Link a gift to a hiding spot (closet, attic, sock drawer). Works with `Tool‑ette #2c` inventory.

### 🧾 Gift History Lookup
Ask: “What did I give them last year?” or “Show 2022 gifts by category.”
Returns formatted summary with tags, feedback, occasion.

### 📬 Idea Reminder Loop
Gently nudges user if something was untagged, not wrapped, or forgotten.
Also supports playful triggers like:
> “What’s in my karma stash?” or “Did I forget something cute in the closet?”
